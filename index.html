    Uses voices installed on your phone, for listening only. Browsers cannot record this speech into the exported video, so exported narration must be your recording or an uploaded MP3 (male/female/English voices from ttsmaker.com).

    Optional AI image / video provider

    Empty by default: everything renders locally. When filled, each scene's prompt is fetched from your URL and used as an animated background (slow zoom for images, looping for videos). The server must return an image or video and allow browser (CORS) requests. Free endpoints such as Pollinations may work this way, but I have not tested any provider. Many good providers need a paid key, and keys typed here stay in your browser.
